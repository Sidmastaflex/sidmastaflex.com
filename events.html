FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can. Weekends fill first, especially in wedding season. A deposit locks your date.

Do you take requests?

Always. Send must-plays and do-not-plays ahead of time, and guests can request on the night.

What do you need from the venue?

A 6×8 ft space, one standard power outlet nearby, and about an hour before guests arrive to set up.

How does payment work?

A deposit holds your date; the balance is due on the event day. Final price is confirmed in writing before you pay anything.

Can you play the music for my culture's traditions?

Yes. Tell me the moments that matter (entrances, dances, rituals) and I'll build the night around them, including custom edits.
